RotMG Progression Guide

How it works. Weapons are ranked by single-target DPS: shots × max(dmg × (0.5 + ATT/50) − DEF, 10%) × (1.5 + 6.5 × DEX/75) × rate of fire, summed over each projectile type; armor-piercing shots ignore DEF. Armor and rings are ranked by stat points (HP ×0.2, MP ×0.1, other stats ×1). Damage abilities (spells, quivers, skulls, scepters…) are ranked by damage per cast; buff/utility abilities (helms, tomes, cloaks…) by stat points, so treat those as a rough guide. ⚠ marks weapons whose spread or alternating pattern makes the all-shots-hit DPS figure unrealistic. They're left out of the suggestions but still listed under "All items". Difficulty uses RealmEye's 0–10 dungeon rating for the easiest non-event dungeon that drops the item. Tiered gear drops everywhere, so it's rated by tier instead. Score = strength on a 0–100 scale, discounted for difficulty (the Prioritize slider sets how much). Checking an item hides everything that scores lower than it. The list then shows the next few items that score higher, easiest first, skipping any that are harder and lower-scoring than another option.
